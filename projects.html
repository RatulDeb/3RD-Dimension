Our engineers

Which engineer does what.

Six branches, one building. Each engineer owns a clear part, so nothing is missed.

Civil Engineering

Civil Engineer — Structure boss

  • Soil test, foundation, columns, beams and slabs
  • Drawings, estimates, site supervision and safety

EEE Engineering

EEE Engineer — Power and safety

  • House and factory wiring, load calculation, DB and breakers
  • Generator, solar, lift power and lightning protection

Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineer — Machines and fittings

  • Excavator, mixer and crane operation and maintenance
  • Water pumps, lifts, steel doors, grills and HVAC

CSE Engineering

CSE Engineer — Design and smart control

  • 3D design, cost software, drone and site progress tracking
  • CCTV, internet, smart home and office automation

Chemical Engineering

Chemical Engineer — Mix and durability

  • Concrete ratio, cement test, water test and curing control
  • Waterproofing, damp-proofing and paint chemistry

MME Engineering

MME Engineer — Steel and materials

  • Steel grade check, welding quality and corrosion guard
  • Right metal for railings, roofing, tanks and bridges
